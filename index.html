A few things to know

Built around practical questions.

Who is RenalTrack for?

Dialysis teams that need an organized way to document dialyzer activity and review new-dialyzer usage against inventory records. The workflow includes technicians, Inventory Specialists, and administrators.

What does an inventory audit record?

The review can capture an audit finding such as Already recorded, along with remarks. Review history helps the team see who checked an entry and when.

What are dialyzer variances?

RenalTrack checks discarded dialyzers for a missing supply pull-out log, a missing first-use (New) log, or a reference, date or model that needs review. Each flag can be traced back to its records and clears once the missing entry is logged. Pull-out or first use on their own never raise a flag.

How does the patient dialyzer allowance work?

Register patients and RenalTrack tracks the free dialyzers each has used in the calendar year. Only first-use (New) logs count, and administrators can record an opening balance with a reason. The default is 32 per year and low-balance alerts begin at 10 remaining. It is a tracking aid, not an eligibility or insurance decision.

What do the notifications cover?

In-app alerts for exhausted or low patient allowances and for discarded dialyzers that need review, each linking to the right screen. Read status is personal to each user and alerts clear once the underlying issue is resolved. Alerts appear inside RenalTrack only.

Can I search records and export reports?

You can search by patient name or ID and use the logbook filters. PDF exports use your selected date range.

Does this website contain live patient information?

No. This is a product showcase. The screenshots and walkthrough use fictional demo records.

How do we evaluate it for our center?

Start by watching the walkthrough and identifying the documentation steps your center wants to improve. A tailored discussion can then cover your workflow, setup, training, and support needs.
